# Therapy for kids 8 and up

Draft for review. Not published.

[Children and teens](/professional/services/children-teens.html) counseling at Open Book is for kids and teens, including play therapy. Every counselor on the team works with children 8 and up and with teens. Some counselors also see children under 8.

The [match](/professional/match.html) tool uses the same age split: a child under 8, a child 8–12, a teen, or an adult.

## What a child's session can draw on

The site describes these approaches in plain language. It does not describe a single session outline for every child.

**Play therapy.** Children use play, their natural language, to work through feelings and experiences with a trained therapist. Offered by Amber, Aspen, and Abby. The services list names play therapy as part of counseling for kids and teens.

**Trauma-Focused CBT.** A structured CBT model for children, teens, and caregivers recovering from trauma, with skills plus a trauma narrative. Offered by Scott, Amber, Shanna, Aspen, and Abby.

**CBT.** Cognitive Behavioral Therapy. Notice the thought-feeling-action loop and practice more helpful thoughts and behaviors. Offered by Scott, Amber, Shanna, Amanda, Aspen, and Abby.

**DBT.** Dialectical Behavior Therapy. Skills for big emotions: mindfulness, distress tolerance, emotion regulation, and relationships. On the children page this is offered by Scott, Amber, Shanna, Amanda, and Aspen.

Those names also appear on the [approaches](/professional/approaches/) page. The explanations live in the [Skill Book](/storybook/#services).

## Medication is a separate visit

Medication management is for ages 8 and up, with Steffani Winter, MSN, APRN, PMHNP-BC. She is a psychiatric mental health nurse practitioner. She is not a psychiatrist or a physician. Only the nurse practitioner prescribes. The counselors do not.

A child does not have to be a therapy client at Open Book to see her, and a therapy client does not have to add medication. She sees children, teens, and adults in Draper and Bountiful and by telehealth. For some medications, an in-person visit may be required. She explains what applies in that situation. The longer note on how therapy and medication can be used together is a separate draft, and the published page is [medication management](/professional/services/medication-management.html).
