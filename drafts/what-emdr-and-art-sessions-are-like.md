# What EMDR and ART sessions are like

Draft for review. Not published.

Open Book Counseling offers trauma-focused, non-shame-based counseling for children, teens, and adults. Two of the approaches named on the [trauma and PTSD](/professional/services/trauma-ptsd.html) page are EMDR and Accelerated Resolution Therapy (ART). The descriptions below are the ones already published in the [Skill Book](/storybook/#services) and on that service page. The site does not publish a minute-by-minute script or a set number of sessions, so this draft does not add one.

## EMDR

EMDR is Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing. It uses guided eye movements or taps while you briefly recall a hard memory, helping your brain re-file it so it feels less raw.

In the words already on the site, an EMDR session is built around that brief recall. The counselor guides eye movements or taps during it. The aim described on the site is that the memory feels less raw, not that you have to retell the whole story from start to finish.

EMDR is listed for Scott Frazier, Amber Frazier, Amanda Lloyd, Aspen Johnson, and Abby Locke. Scott and Amber see people in Draper and Bountiful, and by telehealth. Aspen sees people in Draper and by telehealth. Abby sees people in Draper and Bountiful, and by telehealth. Amanda is telehealth only. Shanna Manjang is listed for trauma and PTSD counseling, and for Trauma-Focused CBT, and is not listed for EMDR or ART.

## ART

Accelerated Resolution Therapy is a short-term, eye-movement-based approach. You can change how a distressing image feels without having to talk through every detail.

That is the session difference the site already draws. Both approaches use eye movements. EMDR is described as eye movements or taps while you briefly recall a hard memory. ART is described as short-term, and as a way to change how a distressing image feels without talking through every detail.

ART is offered by Scott Frazier and Amber Frazier. Both see people in Draper and Bountiful, and by telehealth.

## Who the trauma page lists

Five of the six counselors list trauma and PTSD: Scott, Amber, Shanna, Aspen, and Abby. The same page also names two other approaches, which are not EMDR or ART:

- Trauma-Focused CBT, a structured CBT model for children, teens, and caregivers recovering from trauma, with skills plus a trauma narrative. Offered by Scott, Amber, Shanna, Aspen, and Abby.
- Internal Family Systems. Get to know the parts of you (the protector, the critic, the hurt part) with curiosity instead of shame. Offered by Scott, Amber, Shanna, and Aspen.

Steffani Winter provides medication management. She does not provide EMDR or ART. Those are provided by the counselors.

The same approach names are listed on the [approaches](/professional/approaches/) page, with the explanations kept in the Skill Book.
