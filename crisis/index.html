If you are in crisis

This site is not monitored for emergencies.

  • Call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, 24/7).
  • Utah: SafeUT.
  • Emergency: 911.

988 options

You do not have to be suicidal to contact 988. Call or text 988, or chat at 988lifeline.org.

Veterans, service members, and their families: call 988 and press 1, text 838255, or chat at VeteransCrisisLine.net.

Spanish: call 988 and press 2, text AYUDA to 988, or start a chat in Spanish. For other languages, call 988 and ask for an interpreter. Voice interpretation is available in more than 240 languages.

More places to reach someone

  • The Trevor Project is free, confidential support for LGBTQ+ young people, 24/7. Call 1-866-488-7386, text START to 678-678, or chat on their site.
  • Crisis Text Line: text HOME to 741741 in the United States. It is free, confidential, and available 24/7.
  • NAMI Utah HelpLine, 801-323-9900, is for information and community resources on weekdays during business hours. It is not a crisis line. If you call after hours, they say they return the call within one business day. In a crisis, they direct people to 988.

In Utah, someone can come to you or you can walk in

Call or text 988. In Utah, that call can connect you with the Utah Crisis Line at Huntsman Mental Health Institute. Ask about a Mobile Crisis Outreach Team. Teams in many parts of Utah can meet you in person or by video. HMHI's own mobile team mainly covers Salt Lake County and works with other counties through 988.

Adults 18 and older can walk in 24/7 to the Huntsman Mental Health Institute Crisis Care Center, 955 West 3300 South, South Salt Lake, UT 84119. You can also call 801-583-2500 or 988. Walk-in adult crisis care moved there from the older receiving center.

If a child or teen is in crisis, HMHI lists a youth line at 801-587-7988. If the danger is immediate, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.

For families

You can call or text 988 if you are worried about someone you love. 988's guide for helping someone else says not to keep a plan for suicide a secret, and it lists five steps: ask, be there, help keep them safe, help them connect, and follow up.

SafeUT chats are confidential unless a counselor is worried about someone's safety. In that case they may contact a school or emergency services. Parents, schools, and doctors cannot read the chat. SafeUT is staffed by licensed counselors at Huntsman Mental Health Institute, 24/7, at no cost. Start at safeut.org.

NAMI Utah also offers classes and groups for families. That HelpLine, above, is for information, not for a crisis.

A written safety plan

A safety plan is a short list you fill out before a crisis: warning signs, ways to cope, people to call, and how to make the space safer. SAMHSA publishes a one-page plan modified from Stanley and Brown: 988 safety plan (PDF). The authors' form is at suicidesafetyplan.com/forms.
