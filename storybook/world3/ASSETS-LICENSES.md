## Original pixel art (this repository)

Drawn for this game and included in the same atlas. These are not from the Kenney pack:

- Hero, with a book satchel, four-direction walk frames
- Counselor and villager figures (generic, not likenesses)
- Cat and dog companions
- Symptom clouds (stormy and calm)
- Cottage, sign, lantern, chest, and rock
