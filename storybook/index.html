Chapter I

The Guide-Finding Ritual

Every traveler deserves a trusted guide. Answer the Lantern's five questions, one page at a time. It ranks every guide in the hall and shows its reasons. There's no hidden magic.

Your answers never leave this browser. Research shows the bond with your therapist matters most, so treat this as a starting point.

1. What brings you to the library? (choose any)
2. Who is the story about?
3. Who will be in the room?
4. Any approach you're curious about? (optional)
5. Where would you like to meet?
6. Which office? (optional)
How the Lantern scores (fully transparent)
  • Each concern you choose that a guide lists: +3
  • Age group listed: +2 (not publicly listed: +1, confirm)
  • Format listed: +2 (not listed: +1, confirm)
  • Each preferred approach they use: +1
  • In person or telehealth (if you choose one): offered +2. Amanda Lloyd is telehealth only.
  • Office (if you choose one): sees clients in person there +2. Draper: all except Amanda; Bountiful: Scott, Amber, Abby.
  • Fit % = points ÷ maximum possible for your answers. Every guide is always shown.
