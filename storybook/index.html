Every traveler deserves a trusted guide. Answer the Lantern's five questions, one page at a time. It ranks every guide in the hall and shows its reasons. There's no hidden magic.

Your answers never leave this browser. Research shows the bond with your therapist matters most, so treat this as a starting point.

How the Lantern scores (fully transparent)
  • Each concern you choose that a guide lists: +3
  • Age group listed: +2 (not publicly listed: +1, confirm)
  • Format listed: +2 (not listed: +1, confirm)
  • Each preferred approach they use: +1
  • In person or telehealth (if you choose one): offered +2. Amanda Lloyd is telehealth only.
  • Office (if you choose one): sees clients in person there +2. Draper: all except Amanda; Bountiful: Scott, Amber, Abby.
  • Fit % = points ÷ maximum possible for your answers. Every guide is always shown.

Hi, I'm Abby, an Associate Clinical Mental Health Counselor (ACMHC) at Open Book Counseling. Life can leave us feeling isolated, overwhelmed, or stuck, and whatever you're facing, you don't have to face it alone. Starting therapy is a courageous first step toward healing, growth, and renewed hope. I know that opening up about hard things can feel intimidating, so I work to create a genuine, compassionate, and emotionally safe space where you will be seen, heard, and supported without judgment. I believe healing grows out of authentic connection and a trusting therapeutic relationship. My approach draws on Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), an evidence-based method for understanding how our thoughts, emotions, and behaviors shape one another. My own experiences with difficult life transitions, challenging relationships, trauma, and growing up in a broken home fuel my commitment to walking with others through painful and uncertain seasons. Chapter III · The Spellbook of Approaches

Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing. Uses guided eye movements or taps while you briefly recall a hard memory, helping your brain re-file it so it feels less raw.

Offered by: Scott Frazier, Amber Frazier, Amanda Lloyd, Aspen Johnson, Abby Locke

Accelerated Resolution Therapy. A short-term, eye-movement-based approach where you can change how a distressing image feels without having to talk through every detail.

Offered by: Scott Frazier, Amber Frazier

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy. Notice the thought-feeling-action loop and practice more helpful thoughts and behaviors.

Offered by: Scott Frazier, Amber Frazier, Shanna Manjang, Amanda Lloyd, Aspen Johnson, Abby Locke

Trauma-Focused CBT. A structured CBT model for children, teens and caregivers recovering from trauma, with skills plus a trauma narrative.

Offered by: Scott Frazier, Amber Frazier, Shanna Manjang, Aspen Johnson, Abby Locke

Dialectical Behavior Therapy. Skills for big emotions: mindfulness, distress tolerance, emotion regulation and relationships.

Offered by: Scott Frazier, Amber Frazier, Shanna Manjang, Amanda Lloyd, Aspen Johnson

Internal Family Systems. Get to know the 'parts' of you (the protector, the critic, the hurt part) with curiosity instead of shame.

Offered by: Scott Frazier, Amber Frazier, Shanna Manjang, Amanda Lloyd, Aspen Johnson

Emotionally Focused Therapy. Mostly for couples. Find the cycle you get stuck in and rebuild a secure emotional bond.

Offered by: Scott Frazier, Amber Frazier, Aspen Johnson

Acceptance and Commitment Therapy. Make room for hard feelings and take steps toward what you value.

Offered by: Scott Frazier, Amber Frazier, Amanda Lloyd, Aspen Johnson, Abby Locke

Imago Relationship Therapy. Structured couples dialogue that helps partners really hear each other and the history behind their reactions.

Offered by: Scott Frazier, Amber Frazier

Solution-Focused Brief Therapy. Focuses on goals, strengths and what's already working.

Offered by: Scott Frazier, Shanna Manjang, Aspen Johnson

Play therapy. Children use play, their natural language, to work through feelings and experiences with a trained therapist.

Offered by: Amber Frazier, Aspen Johnson, Abby Locke

A research-based approach for couples that builds friendship, manages conflict, and strengthens connection.

Offered by: Scott Frazier, Amber Frazier, Shanna Manjang, Aspen Johnson, Abby Locke
