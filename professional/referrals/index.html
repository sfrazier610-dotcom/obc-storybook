For hospitals and providers

Refer a client

Hospitals, discharge planners, and other providers can send a client to Open Book Counseling for therapy, medication management, or both.

Use our intake form. Asking for a time does not confirm an appointment. You will usually speak first with Makayla Jones, our admin manager, at (385) 524-6665 or makayla@openbooktherapist.com. Please don't send health details by email.

After a hospital stay

We coordinate step-down care with hospitals so medication and therapy pick up where inpatient care left off.

Scott Frazier, CMHC, CCTP, and Steffani Winter, MSN, APRN, PMHNP-BC, work together with clients who are leaving the hospital after trauma or a suicide attempt. Scott provides the therapy. Steffani provides the medication management.

Steffani sees people ages 8 and up in Draper, Bountiful, and by telehealth. A client does not have to already be in therapy here to meet with her. For some medications, she may need to see the client in person. She will say if that applies. She is being credentialed with the same plans we accept for therapy. Please confirm coverage before the visit.

If someone is in crisis now, call or text 988, use SafeUT, or call 911. This website is not watched for emergencies.
