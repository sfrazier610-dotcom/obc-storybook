About this counselor

Scott Frazier, CMHC, CCTP, is a Licensed Clinical Mental Health Counselor and the co-founder of Open Book Counseling, City Weekly's Best of Utah two years running. Since beginning his career in 1998 in forensic counseling and community mental health, he has also worked in residential treatment and clinical leadership, including as Program Manager at Sierra Tucson, where he was named Employee of the Year in 2019 for strengthening suicide-prevention and client-safety systems. Earlier, he designed a juvenile re-entry program recognized by the Federal Bureau of Prisons as a best practice. Today, Scott helps children (8+), teens, adults, and couples heal from trauma, PTSD, depression, anxiety, addiction, eating disorders, and relationship strain, including clients in high-visibility roles who need discreet care. He's direct, honest, and non-shame-based, and draws on ART, EMDR, CBT, trauma-focused CBT, DBT, EFT, ACT, IFS, Imago, solution-focused therapy, and the Gottman Method. He also mentors and supervises clinicians. He holds a master's in Community Counseling from the University of Phoenix and sees clients in Draper, Bountiful, and online.
