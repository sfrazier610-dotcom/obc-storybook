About Steffani

Steffani is a board-certified psychiatric mental health nurse practitioner who believes medication can be a valuable addition to therapy. Her care is rooted in compassion, collaboration, and meeting you where you are, because treatment should never be one-size-fits-all.

Hello! I'm Steffani, a board-certified Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner at Open Book Counseling, where I provide psychiatric medication management for children (ages 8 and up), teens, and adults in our Draper and Bountiful offices and by telehealth.

I believe medication can be a valuable addition to therapy, and I look forward to partnering with you to make your journey a little easier. I bring a trauma-informed approach to care and have particular experience with ADHD, mood disorders, and adolescent mental health. Treatment should never be one-size-fits-all. Together, we'll explore your concerns, talk through your options, and build a plan that feels right for you.

Reaching out for support isn't always easy. My goal is to create a space where you feel heard, understood, and comfortable being yourself, and I consider it a privilege to walk alongside you as you work toward feeling your best.

Outside of work, I love spending time with my husband, Steven, our children, and our three dogs. We love traveling, exploring new places, and scuba diving whenever we can. For me, life is about balance, new experiences, and making room for what brings us joy.

I look forward to getting to know you!

You do not need to be a therapy client at Open Book to meet with her. Read about medication management.
