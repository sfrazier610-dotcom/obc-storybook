About this counselor

You may feel overwhelmed by anxiety, trauma, or depression while holding everything together for others. On the outside you appear capable, yet inside you feel exhausted from overthinking, emotional pain, or repeating unhealthy relationship patterns. Therapy offers a space to slow down, gain clarity, and understand what is happening beneath the surface. I use evidence based approaches including TF-CBT, DBT, trauma informed care, and the Gottman Method for couples to help you regulate emotions, set boundaries, build resilience, and create lasting change so you can move from surviving to healing.
