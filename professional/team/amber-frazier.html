About this counselor

You may be facing anxiety, depression, trauma, burnout, or low self-worth and feel unsure how to move forward. I help children, teens, adults, couples, and families navigate trauma, abuse, anxiety, depression, and relationship challenges. My specialties include attachment, emotional regulation, foster care, adoption, and substance use. I offer sex-positive, evidence-based care to help you build coping skills, strengthen connection, and create lasting change. I work with clients who may be struggling with depression, anxiety and/or trauma and help you go from survival to thriving. During our initial sessions, we will delve deeply into the issues at hand, and collaborate to create a plan for addressing them in a way that helps you find empowerment to take on and enjoy your life.
