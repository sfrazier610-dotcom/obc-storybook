Policies & FAQ

We're accepting new clients across all of our therapists, in Draper, Bountiful, and online.

We want the practical side of therapy to feel as clear and calm as the sessions themselves. If anything is unclear, call us at 385-524-6665 or email our admin manager at makayla@openbooktherapist.com.

How to get started

  1. Fill out our short intake form. It takes a few minutes.
  2. Our admin manager calls you. She'll answer questions and help match you with a therapist.
  3. Your first appointment. In Draper, in Bountiful, or online.

Not sure yet? We offer a free 15-minute consultation. Ask for one on the intake form or by phone.

Coverage differs by plan and by therapist, so we suggest calling the number on the back of your card too.

Do you offer a sliding scale?

Yes. A sliding scale is available. Ask our admin manager about it when she calls.

What if you're out of network for me?

We can give you a superbill. A superbill is a detailed receipt you can send to your insurance company to ask for out-of-network reimbursement. How much your plan pays back is up to your plan.

What will I owe toward my deductible?

We can't know for sure until a claim runs through your insurance. Your plan decides what applies to your deductible, copay, or coinsurance once it processes the claim.

What if I pay on my own?

If you pay on your own, you have the right to a Good Faith Estimate under the No Surprises Act.

Cancellations and missed sessions

Life happens. If you need to cancel or move a session, please let us know as early as you can so we can offer that time to someone else.

Sessions cancelled less than 24 hours ahead, and missed sessions (no-shows), are charged a $100 fee.

To cancel, call 385-524-6665 or email makayla@openbooktherapist.com.

Telehealth

Online sessions are video sessions from a private space of your choosing. Our admin manager can tell you how to join when your appointment is set.

For the best session, find a quiet, private spot, use headphones if you can, and keep your phone nearby in case the video drops.

Privacy

What you share in therapy is private, and we protect your health information as the law requires. There are a few legal limits to confidentiality, such as when someone's safety is at risk. Your therapist will go over these with you.

This website uses no tracking pixels. Please don't send detailed health information by email or the intake form; save it for your session.

Crisis resources

We are not a crisis service, and this website and our email are not monitored for emergencies. If you need help right now:

  • Call or text 988, the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, 24/7.
  • National Domestic Violence Hotline: call 1-800-799-7233 or text START to 88788, 24/7 (thehotline.org).
  • SafeUT: free 24/7 Utah crisis chat and tip app at safeut.org.
  • Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room if you or someone else is in immediate danger.

More help: Utah and national support resources.
