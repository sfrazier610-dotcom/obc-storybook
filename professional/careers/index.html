Careers · Draper & Bountiful, UT

Join our team

Open Book Counseling is growing. We are looking for clinicians and students who want to do meaningful work in a supportive group practice, with offices in Draper and Bountiful, and telehealth.

Therapists can apply full-time or part-time. Full-time averages 32 clients per week, measured monthly. The nurse practitioner role is part-time to start. Internship and practicum hours follow your program. On the application, please tell us whether you are looking for full-time, part-time, or either, and how many hours you want to work.

Full-time or part-time

Therapists

We are hiring therapists, full-time or part-time, to see clients in Draper, Bountiful, or by telehealth. You would provide individual, couples, family, and group therapy, depending on experience, and complete intakes, treatment plans, and progress notes. Our practice handles billing so you can focus on your clients.

  • Pay: Competitive, DOE (depends on experience)
  • Hours: Full-time or part-time. Full-time averages 32 clients per week, measured monthly · Flexible scheduling
  • Schedule: Office hours are Monday–Friday 8am–9pm and Saturday 8am–4pm. Tell us on the application which days and times work for you, and how many hours you want to work.
  • Employment type: W-2 employee
  • Start date: Immediate
  • Benefits: We cover liability insurance and credentialing
  • Background check: Required
  • Location: Draper, Bountiful, and telehealth
Full description

Responsibilities

  • Provide individual, couples, family, and group therapy, depending on experience.
  • Complete intake assessments, treatment plans, and progress notes on time.
  • Collaborate with our team, including our nurse practitioner as that service grows.
  • Participate in clinical consultation and team meetings.

Qualifications

  • Master's degree in counseling, social work, marriage and family therapy, or a related field.
  • Utah CMHC, LCSW, or LMFT license in good standing.
  • Associates welcome; supervision provided.
  • We have two DOPL-approved supervisors who meet Utah's new supervision requirements.
  • Training in evidence-based or trauma-focused approaches is a plus.
Students

Internship & practicum students

Graduate students in counseling, social work, marriage and family therapy, and psychiatric nurse practitioner programs can earn clinical hours with paid, supervised work on our team. You would see clients as your program allows, complete documentation with supervisor review, and attend supervision and team consultation.

  • Pay: Paid internship · Competitive, DOE (depends on experience)
  • Hours: Based on your program's required hours
  • Schedule: Tell us on the application how many hours you need and which days and times work for you, within office hours (Monday–Friday 8am–9pm and Saturday 8am–4pm).
  • Employment type: W-2 employee
  • Start date: Immediate
  • Benefits: We cover liability insurance and credentialing
  • Background check: Required
  • Location: Draper, Bountiful, and telehealth
Full description

What you'll do

  • Provide therapy to clients under supervision, as allowed by your program.
  • Complete documentation and treatment plans with supervisor review.
  • Attend supervision and team consultation.

Requirements

  • Current enrollment in an accredited graduate program that requires a clinical placement.
  • A placement or affiliation agreement between your school and our practice.
  • Background check required.
  • Supervision is provided by Amber Frazier, CMHC, CCTP, and Scott Frazier, CMHC, CCTP.
Part-time, growing

Nurse practitioner (PMHNP or FNP)

We are adding medication management. Join us as our psychiatric or family nurse practitioner, providing evaluations and medication management for clients referred by our therapists, in Draper, Bountiful, or by telehealth.

  • Pay: Competitive, DOE (depends on experience)
  • Hours: Part-time to start, growing over time · Flexible
  • Schedule: Tell us on the application how many hours you want to work and which days and times work for you, within office hours (Monday–Friday 8am–9pm and Saturday 8am–4pm).
  • Location: Draper, Bountiful, and telehealth
  • Setting: In-person and telehealth
  • Employment type: W-2 employee
  • Start date: Immediate
  • Benefits: We cover liability insurance and credentialing
  • Background check: Required
  • Required to start: Utah APRN license, Utah Controlled Substance license, and DEA registration
  • PMHNP-BC preferred; new graduates welcome

What to expect

Here is how the hiring process works from your side.

  1. Complete the application. It asks which role you want, whether you are looking for full-time, part-time, or either, how many hours you want to work, and which days and times you are available.
  2. Email your resume to makayla@openbooktherapist.com with the position in the subject line. Internship and practicum students, include your program's placement requirements. Nurse practitioners, include a copy of your license(s).
  3. We review applications as they come in and will be in touch if we would like to talk further.
  4. A background check is required if we move forward.

Application

Start your application online. Then email your resume to makayla@openbooktherapist.com with the position in the subject line.

Prefer paper? Download our application, complete it, and email it with your resume to makayla@openbooktherapist.com.

Internship and practicum students: select "Internship / Practicum Student" and include your program's placement requirements. Nurse practitioners: include a copy of your license(s).
