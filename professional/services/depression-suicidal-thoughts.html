Coming home from the hospital

We coordinate step-down care with hospitals so your medication and therapy pick up where inpatient care left off. If you see both a therapist and Steffani, they can share a plan when you agree to it, including when you are stepping down from a hospital stay.

Scott Frazier, CMHC, CCTP, and Steffani Winter, MSN, APRN, PMHNP-BC, work together with clients who are leaving the hospital after trauma or a suicide attempt. Scott provides the therapy. Steffani provides the medication management.

Read more on the medication management page, or use the referral page if a hospital or another provider is sending you.

Therapy and medication together

Steffani Winter, MSN, APRN, PMHNP-BC, is a board-certified psychiatric mental health nurse practitioner. She offers medication management for ages 8 and up in Draper, Bountiful, and by telehealth. You do not have to be a therapy client here to see her. She is the only person here who prescribes. Our counselors do not.

For some medications, she may need to see you in person. She will tell you if that applies to you.

Medication and therapy help in different ways. Medication can ease low mood, panic, or poor sleep, which can make it easier to do the work of therapy. Therapy builds skills and understanding that can last after treatment ends.

For major depression, a review of 52 randomized trials with more than 3,600 adults found that adding therapy to antidepressant medication worked meaningfully better than medication alone, and the benefit lasted up to two years (Cuijpers et al., World Psychiatry, 2014). For chronic depression, 73% of adults improved with medication plus therapy, compared with 48% with either one alone (Keller et al., New England Journal of Medicine, 2000). These findings are about depression. They are not used here for PTSD or GAD.

Common questions

Is this page an emergency service?

No. This site is not monitored for emergencies. If you are in crisis, call or text 988, use SafeUT in Utah, or call 911. More options are on the crisis page.

Who can I see for depression?

You can ask for Scott Frazier, Amber Frazier, Shanna Manjang, or Aspen Johnson.

Do I have to be a therapy client to meet with Steffani Winter?

No. Steffani Winter, MSN, APRN, PMHNP-BC, can see anyone ages 8 and up. You do not need to be in therapy here, and you do not need a referral.

Can therapy and medication be used together for depression?

Yes. For major depression, adding therapy to antidepressant medication worked meaningfully better than medication alone, and the benefit lasted up to two years (Cuijpers et al., 2014). For chronic depression, 73% of adults improved with both, compared with 48% with either one alone (Keller et al., 2000). These findings are about depression. They are not used here for PTSD or GAD.
