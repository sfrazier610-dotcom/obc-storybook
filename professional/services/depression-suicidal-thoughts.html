Therapy and medication management

Medication management is with Steffani Winter, MSN, APRN, PMHNP-BC, a board-certified psychiatric mental health nurse practitioner. She sees people ages 8 and up in Draper and Bountiful and by telehealth. You do not need to be a therapy client at Open Book to meet with her. Only she prescribes medication here. The counselors do not.

For some medications, an in-person visit may be required. She will explain what applies in your situation.

The medication page says medication and therapy help in different ways. Medication can ease symptoms like low mood, panic, or poor sleep, which makes it easier to do the work of therapy. Therapy builds the skills, insight, and healing that last after treatment ends.

A review of 52 randomized trials with more than 3,600 adults found that adding therapy to antidepressant medication worked meaningfully better than medication alone for major depression, panic disorder, and OCD, and the benefit lasted up to two years (Cuijpers et al., World Psychiatry, 2014). In a large trial of adults with chronic depression, 73% improved with medication plus therapy, compared with 48% with either one alone (Keller et al., New England Journal of Medicine, 2000).

Those sentences are the ones already on the medication page. This page uses them for depression. They are not cited here for PTSD or GAD.

We're credentialing Steffani with the same insurance plans we accept for therapy. Credentialing is in progress, so confirm coverage before you come in. Call (385) 524-6665. Plan details are on fees and insurance.
