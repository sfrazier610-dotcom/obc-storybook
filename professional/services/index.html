Fees & insurance

Self-pay: individual $150, couples $200, group $40 per person. Self-pay clients have the right to a Good Faith Estimate under the No Surprises Act.

Insurance accepted by the practice: Aetna, BCBS, Cigna, EMI Health, Evernorth, MultiPlan, Optum, PEHP, Regence, Select Health (incl. Medicare), UnitedHealthcare / UBH / UMR, UHC Global, Medicare, TriWest. EAPs: Intermountain EAP, Optum EAP, Lyra, Select Health EAP.

Credentialing varies by counselor. Please confirm your counselor and benefits with Makayla Jones at (385) 524-6665.
