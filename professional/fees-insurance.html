Questions

What does a session cost?

Self-pay: individual $150, couples $200, group $40 per person.

What if I pay on my own?

If you pay on your own, you have the right to a Good Faith Estimate under the No Surprises Act.

Does medication management use these plans?

Medication management uses the same plans we accept for therapy. We're credentialing Steffani with the same insurance plans we accept for therapy. Because credentialing is in progress, please confirm coverage before you come in. Call us at (385) 524-6665 for current medication visit information.

Do you offer a sliding scale?

Yes. A sliding scale is available. Ask our admin manager about it when she calls.

Do you accept Medicaid?

We do not accept Medicaid.
