Therapy for kids 8 and up

Children and teens counseling at Open Book is for kids and teens, including play therapy. Every counselor on the team works with children 8 and up and with teens. Some counselors also see children under 8.

The match tool uses the same age split: a child under 8, a child 8–12, a teen, or an adult.

Who sees which ages

All six counselors list children and teens: Scott Frazier, Amber Frazier, Shanna Manjang, Amanda Lloyd, Aspen Johnson, and Abby Locke.

Children under 8 are listed for Amber, Amanda, Aspen, and Abby. Scott's published bio names his work with children as 8 and up.

Where they see families is not the same for every counselor:

  • Scott Frazier, CMHC, CCTP: Draper and Bountiful, and telehealth.
  • Amber Frazier, CMHC, CCTP: Draper and Bountiful, and telehealth. The children page says she is well sought out for her trauma work with both children and adults.
  • Shanna Manjang, LCSW: Draper and telehealth.
  • Amanda Lloyd, ACMHC: telehealth only.
  • Aspen Johnson, ACMHC: Draper and telehealth.
  • Abby Locke, ACMHC: Draper and Bountiful, and telehealth.

What a child's session can draw on

The site describes these approaches in plain language. It does not describe a single session outline for every child.

Play therapy. Children use play, their natural language, to work through feelings and experiences with a trained therapist. Offered by Amber, Aspen, and Abby. The services list names play therapy as part of counseling for kids and teens.

Trauma-Focused CBT. A structured CBT model for children, teens, and caregivers recovering from trauma, with skills plus a trauma narrative. Offered by Scott, Amber, Shanna, Aspen, and Abby.

CBT. Cognitive Behavioral Therapy. Notice the thought-feeling-action loop and practice more helpful thoughts and behaviors. Offered by Scott, Amber, Shanna, Amanda, Aspen, and Abby.

DBT. Dialectical Behavior Therapy. Skills for big emotions: mindfulness, distress tolerance, emotion regulation, and relationships. On the children page this is offered by Scott, Amber, Shanna, Amanda, and Aspen.

Those names also appear on the approaches page. The explanations live in the Skill Book.

Medication is a separate visit

Medication management is for ages 8 and up, with Steffani Winter, MSN, APRN, PMHNP-BC. She is a psychiatric mental health nurse practitioner. She is not a psychiatrist or a physician. Only the nurse practitioner prescribes. The counselors do not.

A child does not have to be a therapy client at Open Book to see her, and a therapy client does not have to add medication. She sees children, teens, and adults in Draper and Bountiful and by telehealth. For some medications, an in-person visit may be required. She explains what applies in that situation. The longer note on how therapy and medication can be used together is a separate draft, and the published page is medication management.

Practical details already on the site
