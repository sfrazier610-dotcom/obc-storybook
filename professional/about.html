About us

A practice built on honesty and no shame

Open Book Counseling is a trauma & mental health outpatient group practice in Draper and Bountiful, Utah, and online. We work with individuals, couples, and families, from children and teens through adults, including people in high-stress or high-visibility roles who want discreet, non-shame-based care.

How we work

  • Trauma-focused, non-shame-based therapy.
  • Individual, couples, family, child and teen, and group therapy.
  • In person in Draper and Bountiful, and online.
  • Fit matters. If your first counselor isn't the right fit, let us know and we'll help you switch within the practice.

Where we started

Open Book Counseling was co-founded by Scott Frazier, CMHC, CCTP. Scott began his career in 1998 in forensic counseling and community mental health, and has also worked in residential treatment and clinical leadership. Read Scott's full bio.
