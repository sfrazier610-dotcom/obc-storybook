Support resources

Free and confidential help in Utah and nationwide. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911.

Crisis lines

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988, 24/7 (988lifeline.org).
  • SafeUT: 24/7 chat with a licensed counselor and safety tips, free (safeut.org).
  • Emergency: 911.

Domestic violence: Utah

  • Utah LINKLine (24-hour): call or text 1-800-897-LINK (5465). Free, confidential, statewide; links to shelter, counseling, advocacy, and more. Run by the Utah Domestic Violence Coalition.
  • Utah DCFS domestic violence services: shelters, protective-order help, and treatment programs (dcfs.utah.gov).

Domestic violence: national

  • National Domestic Violence Hotline: 1-800-799-7233, text START to 88788, or chat at thehotline.org. 24/7.
  • StrongHearts Native Helpline: 1-844-762-8483, for Native Americans and Alaska Natives.
  • National Teen Dating Abuse Helpline: 1-866-331-9474 (loveisrespect.org).

Legal help in Utah

  • Utah Legal Services: free civil legal aid, including family safety. Apply online or call 801-328-8891 (utahlegalservices.org).
  • Utah Legal Help: directory of free, reduced-cost, and do-it-yourself legal help. Call or text 801-297-7053 (utahlegalhelp.org).
  • Utah Courts self-help: protective orders, stalking injunctions, and step-by-step forms (MyPaperwork) (utcourts.gov).

Internet use can be monitored. If you're worried, use a safer device or call instead.
